01 · The operating model

Your training, run like a mission

Fighter squadrons don't rely on motivation. They rely on a cycle that turns every sortie into progress. Your training runs on the same loop.

PHASE / A

Brief

Zones set in TrainingPeaks, session targets defined, pacing and fuelling planned before you start. You never guess what today is for.

PHASE / B

Execute

Structured sessions with clear intensity discipline. Easy days stay easy, hard days hit their numbers, and the plan survives contact with real life.

PHASE / C

Debrief

Every race and key session analysed from the data file: planned vs. happened vs. why vs. what changes. No ego, no excuses — just the next correction.
